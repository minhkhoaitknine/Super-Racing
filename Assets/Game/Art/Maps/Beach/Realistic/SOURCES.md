Downloaded from Poly Haven at 2K resolution. License: CC0.

- Coast Sand 01, Rob Tuytel: https://polyhaven.com/a/coast_sand_01
- Coast Sand Rocks 02, Rob Tuytel: https://polyhaven.com/a/coast_sand_rocks_02

Generated meshes change visual UVs/normals only; gameplay colliders retain the original meshes.
